## So `re/leads.json` now holds what has been NOTICED AND NOT YET ANSWERED

    re/ledger.json    what the project BELIEVES, with the grade of evidence

    **re/leads.json   what the project has NOTICED AND NOT YET ANSWERED**

**and the field that makes it a queue rather than a notebook is `settled_by`**: a lead must say WHAT WOULD CLOSE IT, **because a question nobody has said how to close is a
topic and not a task.** This session produced one of those -- "what do +0x08 and +0x0C mean" stood for two rounds until a constructor preamble answered it, **and until then
nothing said which observation would count.**

## Four commands, and the third is the one that matters

    add      a lead recorded when it is noticed, with the evidence that produced it
    sweep    **candidates DERIVED from the tree** -- the ledger's witnesses and the headers' comments, matched against the phrasings this repository actually uses for
             an open question (`is not established`, `is unresolved`, `has not been read`, and eight more)
    next     the highest-scoring open lead, so a round can START from the queue instead of from memory
    resolve  closed, with what closed it

**`sweep` IS THE PART THAT MAKES IT SYSTEMATIC RATHER THAN A NOTEBOOK, because it finds the ones that were never added.** It reports **32 distinct sources** -- including
`engines.hpp`'s common-base-or-template question, `nester.hpp`'s four-way contradiction, `engines_composite.hpp`'s begin/end question and `base_chain.hpp`'s vector element
type. **Candidate sentences are counted by their SOURCE and not by their number**, because one header line can hold two open clauses and counting sentences made a covered
queue look short.

**And scoring puts a lead the HUMAN raised above one an inference produced**, then prefers one with a real closing condition, **because that is the one that can be finished.**

## The gate holds two invariants and reports one budget

`re/gate.ps1` gained a `leads` stage, and it refuses exactly two things: **a lead with no evidence pointer** (a lead that cannot say what produced it is a memory) and **a
queue smaller than what the tree's sweep finds** (a question written into a header and not into the queue is a lead that gets lost). **The 38 leads that still carry a DERIVED
closing condition are REPORTED with their names and a target of zero** -- **the same treatment `forwardedCount` gets, where what is gated is that the number must not
rise.** Making them fail the gate would stop the work; letting them pass silently is the thing the check exists to prevent.

**AND THE FIRST VERSION OF THE CHECK REFUSED THEM ALL, WHICH WOULD HAVE LEFT THE GATE RED** -- so the invariant and the budget were separated deliberately, and both are
stated in `re/RULES.md` with the number visible.
