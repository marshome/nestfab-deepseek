offsets is a base and not a coincidence**, so the range 0x00 to 0x50 belongs to `Engine::Engine`, **and the port declared it empty.**

**AND THAT IS WHY THE MEMBERS GO ON THE BASE AND NOT ON `DelayedEngine`** -- which is where last round put them, from `DelayedEngine`'s own body. **The evidence was
