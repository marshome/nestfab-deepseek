**THE IDENTITY IS THE VTABLE AND NOT THE NAME.** `re/vtables.json` has `N5Multi19SupervisorCancellerE` at vtable 0xA3BA90 with slots `0x69A6D0 0x69A690 0x30030`, **so

**AND THE CONSTRUCTOR `0x30A30` (187 bytes) SAYS WHAT +0x10 IS**: `0x30A4C lea rcx, [rcx + 0x20]` then `0x30A50 mov qword ptr [rbx + 0x10], rcx` -- **a `std::string`'s
data pointer aimed at its own inline buffer 0x10 bytes further on** -- with the length at +0x18, the small-string byte at +0x20, and the size compared against `0xf` at
`0x30A70`. **So the object is a vptr, a sink at +0x08, an inline string, and a byte flag at +0x30.**

## And two things this round did NOT resolve, both recorded rather than smoothed

**FIRST, WHERE THE BASE'S DATA BEGINS IS NOT SHOWN BY ANY INSTRUCTION READ.** `0x30A30` is the DERIVED class's constructor **and it never calls a base constructor**: its
first two stores install the vtable and write its own first member. **An attempt to make `Canceller::cancelled_` land on +0x30 by padding the base HAD TO BE REVERTED**,
because the padding landed on top of the derived class's own +0x08 and +0x10 -- **a second wrong layout rather than one right one.** `Canceller` keeps the flag where the
other three cancellers are consistent with, **and its note says the +0x30 is the thing to explain.**

**SECOND, THE PROBE'S BRANCH IS NOT REPRODUCED.** Both operands come from the sink (`0x5F3980` produces the elapsed value and `[sink + 0x408]` the limit), **and this port
has no such sink** -- so `SupervisorCanceller::probeCancel` does the two things it can reach, the null short-circuit and the flag, **and says the division is not
reproduced rather than approximating it with a clock of its own.**

## And `Engine::run` keeps its deadline through a class that is MARKED substituted

`TimeLimitCanceller` is new and `LCNS_SUBSTITUTED` in prose: **the comparison is the module's (`0x3008A divsd` then `0x30092 ucomisd`) and the clock it divides is the
port's.** `engine.cpp` was wiring the old `TimeCanceller` for a real timeout, so removing it without a replacement would have silently dropped the deadline.
